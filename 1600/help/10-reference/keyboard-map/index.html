Keyboard map

How the keys of your computer's keyboard reach the machine's keys. The map is the same in the Qt6 frontend and in the Web frontend, and for every model; where a model lacks a key, the table says what happens instead. Typing into the faceplate, Paste Text and a preset's typed text follow the same character rules (Getting started, shared Getting started). "Shift+X" in a column of a model means that the machine's own SHIFT is tapped first, then X.

Accented characters (PC-1600)

The PC-1600's KB II key latches, and while it is on the letters and ( ) give the characters below (ROM tables KYCDKB2 / KYCDSK2, bank 6 9592H / 95E5H; CP437 codes). A character from this list, typed on your keyboard, is typed as KB II, [SHIFT,] key, KB II.

  • Your keyboard, Paste Text and a preset's typed text all keep the case: ä gives ä, Ä gives Ä. This is unlike the plain letters, which your keyboard types in uppercase. An uppercase the PC-1600 lacks (Ë, Û) gives its lowercase.
  • With SHIFT latched on the machine, it is released first and the character is typed as usual. With KB II latched, an accented key of your keyboard does nothing: press the letter itself instead (R gives Ö).
  • SML doesn't change these characters.
  • Dead keys compose as usual (¨ then U gives Ü on a Swiss or German layout, Option-U then U on a US Mac): the product takes part in the system's input method and types the composed character.

Modifiers

  • macOS: anything held with Cmd or Control never reaches the machine (shortcuts). Option still types the character it produces.
  • Windows/Linux: anything held with the Windows/Super key, or with Ctrl or Alt alone, never reaches the machine. Ctrl+Alt together (AltGr) still types the character it produces.
  • Shift tapped on its own — pressed and released within 0.4 s, with no other key or mouse click in between — taps the machine's SHIFT, latching it for the next key. Shift held while typing another key only acts as a modifier.

Not mapped

ON and OFF have no key on your keyboard; click them on the faceplate.
