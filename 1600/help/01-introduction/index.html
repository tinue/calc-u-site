Chapter 1 · Introduction

Introduction

Calc-U-1600 emulates three Sharp pocket computers: the PC-1500, the PC-1500A and the PC-1600. Around them it emulates the memory modules, the CE-150 and CE-1600P plotters with their cassette interfaces, the CE-1600F floppy drive and the CE-158 interface. It connects the computers' serial ports to your computer, and on the PC-1600 a folder on your computer can be a drive.

Calc-U-1600 runs as a desktop program on macOS, Linux and Windows, and in the browser (The browser version). This help describes both; where the browser differs, it says so.

The window has three parts:

  • The faceplate (the keys and the LCD) at the top.
  • The control bar below it: the model, the memory slots and the peripherals.
  • The panels at the bottom: the INSPECTOR and the plotter's paper, plus the CE-158's printer output while a CE-158 is plugged in.

Everything you can plug into the real computers, Calc-U-1600 can plug in too:
