Chapter 5 · Peripherals and paper

Plotters and the CE-158

Places, attaching and detaching, the paper panel with Copy and Cut are the family's; the shared part of this chapter describes them. This part covers Calc-U-1600's peripherals: the CE-150 and CE-1600P plotters and the CE-158 interface.

Peripheral Models What it is
CE-150 PC-1500, PC-1500A, PC-1600 Four-colour pen plotter with a cassette interface
CE-1600P PC-1600 Four-colour pen plotter with a cassette interface and the CE-1600F floppy drive
CE-158 PC-1500, PC-1500A, PC-1600 Interface with a serial (RS-232C) and a printer (parallel) port

On the PC-1600 the CE-150 is a plotter only: its cassette interface is not available there.

They are plugged in and out with the control bar (the CE-150 and CE-158 buttons on the PC-1500 and PC-1500A; the printer picker and the CE-158 button on the PC-1600) or in Machine ▸ Peripherals (More ▸ Peripherals on mobile, where the bar has none of these controls). One plotter at a time can be plugged in. The CE-1600P and the CE-158 cannot be used together: plugging in one while the other is there is refused with a notice.

On the real computers the ROM only notices a new peripheral when it powers on, so plugging in or out goes through a power cycle:

sequenceDiagram
    actor You
    participant App as Calc-U-1600
    participant M as Computer
    You->>App: CE-150 / CE-1600P
    App->>M: power off
    App->>M: plug the plotter in (or out)
    App->>M: power on (at full speed)
    M->>M: the ROM finds the plotter, sets the pen up
    Note over You,M: ready at the prompt, the plotter live

The plotters

The paper panel is always there; it shows the plotter's name while one is plugged in, and empty paper without a name while none is. Each plotter keeps its own paper until you cut it or load a preset that plugs it in: through unplugging, a ROM or module change, a model switch (also from a PC-1500 to a PC-1600) and a Reset or Cold boot, after which the next plot goes on below what is drawn. A paper feed without a mark shows as blank paper. The paper scrolls, and it follows the pen as long as you are at the bottom. Copy and Cut are the two icons at the right of the row below the paper (two sheets, scissors):

Copy puts the whole plot on the clipboard at its physical size, ready to paste or print:

Cut copies the plot, then tears the paper off, so that the next plot starts on a clean sheet.

The CE-1600P has its own ROM, New (the default) or Old, which the CE-1600F floppy disk drive uses too. The control bar and the menus plug it in with the New ROM; a preset or the harness can name the Old one. Machine ▸ CE-1600P ROM (while it is plugged in) switches to the Old one and back, keeping the disk in the drive, the same side up, and the cassette. Unplugging the CE-1600P takes the disk out of the CE-1600F.

The CE-1600P has a cassette recorder, and so has the CE-150 on the PC-1500 and PC-1500A (not on the PC-1600): while one with a recorder is plugged in, the control bar shows the cassette picker and the counter (Loading a BASIC program); unplugging it takes the cassette out.

The CE-158

The CE-158 button or Machine ▸ Peripherals ▸ CE-158 plugs in the CE-158 interface, with a power cycle like the plotters. It works on all three models.

  • Serial port: the CE-158's port is a serial port file of your computer, calcu1600-ce158.serial, in the link folder (desktop only; COM ports has how to use it and the settings).
  • Printer port: whatever a program prints on the parallel port (OPN "LPRT", then LPRINT) appears in the CE-158 panel. Save… writes it to a file exactly as it was sent; Clear starts a new sheet.

In BASIC:

  • OPN "LPRT" sends LPRINT to the printer port, and OPN alone switches it back.
  • SETDEV PO sends LPRINT and LLIST to the serial port instead, and SETDEV KI makes INPUT read from it.
  • OUTSTAT 0 switches the DTR and RTS lines on.

The full command set is in the CE-158 reference.

Known gap: on the PC-1600, SETDEV KI with INPUT doesn't work yet; RINKEY$ does.
