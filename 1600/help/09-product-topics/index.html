Chapter 9 · Product topics

Product topics

What only Calc-U-1600 has:

  • What differs by model: the ROM variants, and what a Reset, a power cycle and a Cold boot keep on the PC-1500, PC-1500A and PC-1600.
  • BASIC programs and the cassette: loading a BASIC listing, a tokenized program or a cassette WAV; CLOAD and CSAVE on a cassette.
  • Machine-code programs: loading a .bin, where it goes, and how to start it.
  • COM ports: the PC-1600's RS-232C port and the CE-158 as serial port files on your computer.
  • A folder as a drive: a folder on your computer as the PC-1600's drive S3:.
  • PC-1500 keyword modules: how a ROM adds BASIC commands to the PC-1500.
  • Module definitions: describing a memory module of your own.
