Calc-U 1600

Calc-U-1600 emulates three Sharp pocket computers, the PC-1500, PC-1500A and PC-1600, together with their memory modules, the CE-150 and CE-1600P plotters with their cassette interfaces, the CE-1600F floppy drive and the CE-158 interface. It runs on macOS, Linux and Windows, and in your browser.

What Calc-U-1600 plugs in

  • Three models, PC-1500, PC-1500A and PC-1600, switched from the control bar (introduction).
  • Memory modules in the PC-1500's slot or the PC-1600's two (modules and disks).
  • The CE-150 and CE-1600P plotters with their cassette interfaces, and the CE-1600F floppy drive (peripherals).
  • The CE-158 and the PC-1600's own RS-232C port, connected to your computer (COM ports).
  • On the PC-1600, a folder on your computer as the drive S3: (host drive).
  • A debugger in VS Code for BASIC and machine code (debugger).
