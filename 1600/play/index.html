Calc-U-1600 online

Above is the Calc-U-1600 emulator, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser. Machine ▸ Model switches between the PC-1500, the PC-1500A and the PC-1600; the control bar below the faceplate plugs in the modules, the plotters and the floppy drive. File ▸ Examples opens the bundled programs, File ▸ Load Preset… your own. Files are read in your browser and never leave your machine.

Your saved modules, disks and cassettes stay in your browser from one visit to the next (File ▸ Saved Media…). What the browser version has and has not, compared with the desktop program, is in The browser version.

Prefer it without the page around it? Open the standalone app: the same emulator alone, installable as an app that runs offline. For the desktop program, see the downloads.
