Chapter 9 · Product topics

What differs by model

Reset, Cold boot and the power cycle after a change of hardware are the family's (Models and machine control). What each of them keeps depends on the model:

TI-59, TI-58 TI-58C
Reset (Machine ▸ Reset) the program, the data registers and the partition kept; everything else as after switching on the same as a power cycle
Power cycle (a module chosen, the PC-100C attached or detached) the memory lost: as the ROM's start-up leaves a calculator whose memory was lost the constant memory kept (all of its memory, H00 to H03 included); everything else as after switching on
Cold boot (Machine ▸ Cold boot) all memory zero, then the ROM's start-up the same, the constant memory included

"Everything else as after switching on" means, among other things: a running program has stopped, and a library program that was selected no longer is.

Through all three, on every model:

  • the PC-100C and the module stay plugged in;
  • the cue card that is fitted stays, and a fitted magnetic card can be fed again (Magnetic cards); a card in the reader at that moment is taken out as by Eject Card;
  • the printer's TRACE switch keeps its position;
  • an ASM overlay stops running; a Cold boot also removes it (Debugger).

A model switch, and every preset, builds a new calculator from a cold boot. A TI-58C built at a start or a model switch without a startup preset gets its constant memory back from its file (Constant memory).

The models side by side

TI-59 TI-58 TI-58C
Memory 960 steps or 100 registers 480 steps or 60 registers 480 steps or 60 registers, kept when switched off; H00 to H03
Partition after switching on 479.59 239.29 239.29
Card reader yes no no
Modules, the PC-100C yes yes yes
Clock regular regular about 15 % slower, as the real one

A card fed to a TI-58 or TI-58C, by a drop or a preset, is refused: they have no card reader. Registers 60 to 99 exist on the TI-59 only.
