Chapter 9 · Product topics

Cue cards

A cue card is the card in the window above the top row of keys. It labels the user-defined keys A to E and A′ to E′ for the program that is loaded, as the cards of the real calculator did. Fitting a cue card changes nothing in the calculator.

Three kinds

Kind Comes from Looks like
Stand-alone cue card a preset's cue-card: section; on the TI-58C, the constant memory file a plain card written by hand
Magnetic card (TI-59) a card file, or an in-file card of a preset (Magnetic cards) a card with a magnetic strip; each top corner shows the bank of its side
Module cue card the module in the slot, one per library program (Solid State ROM modules) pre-printed, in gold

The window shows one card at a time. Your own card is the stand-alone cue card or the magnetic card fitted last; a new one takes the place of the one before. While a library program is selected (2nd Pgm nn), its module cue card lies over your own card, and your card shows again when no library program is selected.

  • A preset builds a new calculator, which has no card until the preset fits one.
  • A Reset, a power cycle and a Cold boot keep the fitted card.
  • A magnetic card without a cue card of its own shows a plain card titled with its name.
  • A magnetic card always carries its own cue card, never the preset's cue-card:.

Writing a cue card

A cue card is a block of keys, in a preset's cue-card: section, in an in-file card of magnetic-cards: or in a card file:

cue-card:
  title: Roots & Powers
  A: \sqrt x
  B: x^{2}
  C: 1/x
  D: x \to y
  E: \blank
  "A'": \sum x_{i}
  "B'": \xbar
  "C'": n

Key Value
title the headline
A … E the labels of the A–E row
A' … E' the labels of the A′–E′ row, written in quotes: "A'": text
row-1 one line of text in place of the A′–E′ labels
row-2, row-2-r one line of text in place of the A–E labels; row-2-r stands right of it
row-1-align left, center (the default) or right
row-2-align, row-2-r-align left (the default), center or right
style none (the default) or button: a box around the row's text
pencil-color black (the default), pencil or sharpie, for the whole card
  • \blank as a label is an empty cell that the label before it spans into: D: x \to y and E: \blank give one label over D and E. An empty label is an empty cell nothing spans into.
  • A label row shows only while its row text is empty.
  • Shortcuts give the signs real cards used: \sqrt √, \xbar x̄, \to →, \sum ∑, \pi π, ^{2} ², _{i} ᵢ and more; the whole table is in Media files.
  • A text that contains : or #, or starts with [, { or a quote, is written in quotes.

The tutorial builds a card step by step (Preset tutorial). To change the card of a saved magnetic card, edit the cue-card: block of its card file in a text editor; the change shows the next time the card is fitted.
