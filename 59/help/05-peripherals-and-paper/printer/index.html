Chapter 5 · Peripherals and paper

The PC-100C printer

Calc-U-59 has two places for peripherals on every model: the printer place, where the PC-100C print cradle goes, and the module slot, where a Solid State ROM module goes (Solid State ROM modules). What every product does with peripherals and paper is in the shared part of this chapter; this part shows the PC-100C.

Attaching the printer

The button PC-100C at the left of the paper panel's header attaches and detaches the printer; it shows the printer, pressed while it is attached (on the iPhone and iPad its dot turns red). Machine ▸ Peripherals ▸ and a preset's printer: PC-100C do the same. Without a startup preset, Calc-U-59 starts without the printer.

Attaching or detaching the PC-100C goes through a power cycle, as plugging the real calculator into the cradle does: the TI-59 and TI-58 lose their program and data registers, the TI-58C keeps its constant memory (What differs by model).

The printer's controls

While the PC-100C is attached, the paper panel has the printer's own controls, at the left of the row below the paper:

  • PRINT prints the display; in a narrow panel it reads PRN.
  • T is the printer's TRACE switch; its dot is lit while it is on. While it is on, the calculator prints every step it carries out. The switch keeps its position when the printer is detached and attached again, and through a model switch, as the real switch does. A preset sets it with printer-trace: on; without that step a preset leaves it off.
  • ADV advances the paper while you hold it.

The paper

The paper panel shows the PC-100C's paper in one of two views; the control that switches them shows the view that is on:

  • the dots, as the thermal printer put them on the paper, faint dots included;
  • the text, which can be selected and read quickly.

Copy (the two sheets at the right of the row) puts the paper on the clipboard as a picture and as text; Cut (the scissors) copies it and then empties it (Copy and Cut). For long output, copy it and paste it into a text editor.

While the PC-100C is detached, the panel shows an empty paper without a name; the paper is back when you attach the printer again.

Speed

The printer runs at the real PC-100's speed, measured on a TI-59 and a TI-58C: a printed line takes about 0.29 s on the TI-59 (a 2nd List line 0.31 s), a blank line from ADV about 0.19 s. The printer runs on the calculator's clock, so on the TI-58C, whose clock is slower, everything prints about 15 % slower, as on the real one. Programs that use the printer interrupt to cut a line short print the same dots as the real printer (the article on the printer interrupt).

Holding the display runs the calculator at full speed, printing included: a long 2nd List finishes in moments. A held ADV feeds the same lines at every speed.
