Chapter 10 · Reference

Keyboard map

Your computer's keyboard presses the calculator's keys by this map, the same on every model and in every frontend that takes keys from a keyboard: the Mac, Windows and Linux apps and the browser. In the browser, the calculator takes the keys while it has the focus: click it once; click elsewhere, or press Tab, and the page gets the keyboard back.

  • A letter is the letter your key carries on your keyboard layout, so the map follows the layout you use; every other character is the one your layout produces for the key.
  • No other key reaches the calculator: 2nd, INV, STO, RCL, LRN, R/S and the rest are pressed on the faceplate (or in a preset, by their key IDs: Preset sections).
  • A key held with Command, Control, Option or Alt, or the Windows key, is never the calculator's: it is a shortcut of the menus or of the system.
  • Your keyboard's auto-repeat is ignored. A key is held at least 100 ms of emulated time and until the ROM has read it, also when you let go sooner; two keys held at once reach the calculator together.
