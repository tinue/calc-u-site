Chapter 4 · Media

Magnetic cards

Calc-U-59's medium is the magnetic card of the TI-59; the TI-58 and the TI-58C have no card reader. What every medium of the family does (templates and instances, the save folder, Name & Save, opening and dropping files) is in the shared part of this chapter; this part shows the card.

A magnetic card has a name, a cue card and two sides, 0 and 1, as the real card has two. One card file holds both sides. A side is empty, or holds one bank: a quarter of the calculator's memory, with the partition it was written at, whether it is program or data, and whether it is protected (Magnetic cards and banks). Either side can hold any bank, even both the same one.

Fitting and feeding

Using a card takes two steps:

  1. Arm it: it is fitted into the calculator above the keys, where it labels them with its cue card. Nothing is read or written yet, and the calculator sees nothing of it.
  2. Feed a side by pressing a top corner of the card: the top left corner feeds side 0, the top right corner side 1. The corner lights up while the side passes the reader. The calculator reads the side, or writes it when it waits for a card to write. The card stays fitted afterwards, so the other side is one press away.

Each corner shows the number of the bank on its side, or nothing while the side is empty; the number follows a write.

Saving a program

  1. Press the Crd button with the plus sign, Arm Fresh Card (File ▸ Arm Fresh Card…), and give the card a name. The fresh card is fitted above the keys, its cue card titled with its name. If a card of that name is saved, the dialog asks "Replace card <name>?" first: its first write replaces both sides and the cue card.
  2. Press 2nd Write for the bank you want to save: 1 2nd Write for bank 1.
  3. Press a corner of the card. Once the side has passed, it is saved in the card's file and the corner shows the bank it now holds.

A program too long for one side continues on the other: 2 2nd Write, then the other corner. Nothing is written to the save folder until the fresh card's first write: arming a fresh card is Name & Save done up front.

Loading it back

  1. Press the other Crd button, Arm Card (File ▸ Arm Card…), and choose the card. The dialog lists your saved cards by their names; with none saved it says "No cards found". On a computer and in the browser you can also drop a card file on the window.
  2. Press the corner of the side you want. While the calculator waits for a key it reads the side by itself, as the real one does: the bank is stored when the card's partition is the calculator's and its checksum is right, and the display blinks otherwise.

Good to know

  • Eject Card takes the place of the Crd buttons while a side is in the reader. It takes the card out at once; the card stays fitted. A bank is kept only when the calculator had written all of it.
  • A library program's cue card and a fitted card lie on top of each other, the one put there last on top; the corners do nothing while the program's card covers yours. Fitting a card brings it on top, and so does 2nd Pgm 00, which selects no library program.
  • A fitted card stays through a Reset, a power cycle and a Cold boot, and can be fed again afterwards. It goes when you fit another one, switch the model or load a preset. Its file stays as it is.
  • While a card is in the reader, no other card can be fitted.
  • Only the side you write changes. The card's name, its description, its cue card and the other side stay as the file holds them.
  • A card the calculator does not take at once, while a program runs that asks for none, stays at the reader until the calculator takes it.
  • A program that asks for a card (n Write, n INV Write in the program) waits until you feed a side or press R/S.
  • A card on a TI-58 or TI-58C is refused with a notice.

Where cards live

Cards are files <name>.magnetcard.yaml in the save folder (magnetic card format 3, Media files), named after the card:

System Save folder
iPhone, iPad the app's iCloud storage (iCloud Drive ▸ Calc-U-59), or its local Documents folder when iCloud is off
macOS, Windows, Linux Calc-U-59 in your home folder, unless Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Save folder names another
the browser the browser's storage for the page, from one visit to the next; Import Media… brings a card file in, Saved Media… lists, downloads and deletes cards

A card file opened from elsewhere, by a drop or with Open With, is used and written back where it lies; in the browser it is imported into the save folder first. A card file is plain text: its cue card can be edited in a text editor (Cue cards), and the next time the card is fitted it shows the change.

Presets can feed cards and arm them, define cards of their own and save them (Presets of this product).
